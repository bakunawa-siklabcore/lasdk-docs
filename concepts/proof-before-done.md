# A completion message is a starting point

An AI assistant can say that work is complete. The person responsible still needs to understand what changed, what was checked, and what may remain open before deciding whether to accept it.

LASDK is designed to make that review clearer by keeping relevant work and delivery information together. Its output can help surface questions that need attention, but it is not a warranty, certification of safety, or approval to release software.

People remain responsible for checking that the result fits their needs and for deciding whether to accept or release it.
