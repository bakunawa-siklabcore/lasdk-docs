# From a request to a reviewable result

LASDK is being built to help teams carry a software request through AI-assisted work and arrive at a result they can inspect and discuss.

At a customer level, the idea is simple: describe the change to your AI coding assistant, let it work with LASDK as part of delivery, then review the result and any open questions before deciding what to do with it.

The precise steps and available capabilities depend on the supported beta release. We will provide operating instructions to invited participants. This overview describes the intended experience, not a guarantee that every task can be completed without intervention.
