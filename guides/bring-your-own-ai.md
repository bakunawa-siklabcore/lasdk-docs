# Use the AI tools that fit your work

LASDK is designed to work with AI coding assistants, with the assistant acting as the operator for the requested work. During closed beta, only the tools and configurations explicitly confirmed for that build are supported. Ask Siklab for the current list before connecting a tool.

Your AI provider's data practices depend on the provider, the coding tool, and the settings you choose. Review those terms and controls before using project information with an AI service.

Do not assume that every assistant, model, or provider is compatible. Support may vary by tool version and configuration.
