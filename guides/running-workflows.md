# Working with LASDK

In the intended workflow, you tell your AI coding assistant what outcome you need. The assistant works with LASDK to carry out the supported delivery work, then reports the result and questions that need your attention. You remain responsible for reviewing the change and deciding what to accept.

The precise actions available depend on your beta build and configuration. Follow the instructions supplied with that build; this page describes the user-facing model, not a command reference or a promise that all work will run unattended. For access information, see the [product overview](../index.md).
