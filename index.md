# LASDK — software delivery for AI-assisted development

**Let AI build. Know when the work is ready.**

## What we're building

LASDK is a software-delivery product from Siklab for work done with AI coding assistants. Siklab Core is a separate product that uses LASDK; LASDK is also being developed for use on its own. LASDK is preparing for a closed beta and is not generally available yet.

## The problem it addresses

AI can produce software quickly, while the person responsible still needs to understand the request, the result, the checks, and the open questions. A confident completion message alone may leave important questions unanswered.

## How it helps

The person describes the work to an AI coding assistant. The assistant can use LASDK as part of carrying that work through delivery. LASDK is designed to help keep the request, relevant project context, checks, review, and the resulting work connected, so a person can see what happened and what still needs attention.

## Expected outcome

A more informed decision about whether to revise, accept, or continue the work. LASDK does not guarantee that software is correct, secure, complete, or production-ready; people remain responsible for acceptance and release decisions. Specific beta features and supported tools will be published when verified.

## Availability

LASDK is in active development and is not yet available for general use. Closed-beta access and supported tools will be confirmed with invited participants. For enquiries, contact [Siklab Core](mailto:lasdk@siklabcore.com).
